# Ledger

* [Accounts and cards](accounts-and-cards.md) - Cash and bank accounts, and credit cards whose billing cycle and limit move on settlement.
* [Transactions](transactions.md) - Income, expense, and transfer plans with a recurrence shape fixed at creation.
* [Occurrence projection](occurrence-projection.md) - A month's statement is a SQL projection of occurrences, not a stored table.
* [Settlements](settlements.md) - Daily processing posts due occurrences and card invoices in order, one open month at a time.
* [Imports](imports.md) - CSV rows become transactions through a preview, ordered rules, and one job per row.
